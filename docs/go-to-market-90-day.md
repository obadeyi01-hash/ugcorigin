# UGCOrigin — 90-day go-to-market (launch-first, no waitlist)

> **Status:** v1 — 2026-10-17.
> **Principle [LOCKED]:** **No waitlist.** Waitlists are dead weight in 2026 — they leak momentum and let someone beat us to market. We launch a working product to a small real cohort and bring people in *live*, from day one. Speed to market > collecting emails.
> **Sequence [LOCKED]:** supply first (creators), then demand (brands), with concierge matching in the middle so early brands have a great first experience.

## The shape of it
- **Weeks 1–4:** build the launchable core + recruit the first creator cohort in parallel.
- **Weeks 5–8:** open creator sign-ups publicly, hit supply density in 3–4 niches, start concierge brand outreach.
- **Weeks 9–12:** bring brands in, run the first real escrow-protected gigs, collect reviews, iterate, and prime repeat business.

---

## Phase 1 — Weeks 1–4: Build core + seed supply
**Product (visible MVP):** auth (Google/Meta/X), creator onboarding + verification gate, creator directory + profiles + Jolts, Direct-Hire (Path A), gig workspace (upload, time-stamped feedback, approve), escrow + Paystack payout, two-way reviews, rules-based ranking, core analytics. (Everything else built but flagged off.)

**Recruit the first 50–100 creators (hands-on, no waitlist):**
- DM/outreach creators already posting product content on **TikTok/IG/X** (search #UGCcreator, #UGCNigeria, #Naijacontentcreator, unboxing/testimonial content).
- Offer: **0% fees forever, featured placement, and a few seeded/guaranteed gigs you fund yourself** to prove payouts work.
- Onboard them personally — help build strong profiles, set rates, link socials, pass verification.
- Target niches first: **beauty/skincare, fintech, food, e-commerce, lifestyle, SaaS/tech.**

**Success check (end of W4):** 50+ verified creators live across ≥4 niches; 5–10 seeded gigs completed end-to-end (so the escrow→delivery→payout→review loop is battle-tested before brands arrive).

## Phase 2 — Weeks 5–8: Density + concierge demand
**Supply:** open public creator sign-ups. Push to **150–300 creators**. Launch a **creator referral loop** (invite creators → perks/tier boost). Keep verification stringent.

**Demand (concierge, not self-serve yet):**
- Hand-pick **20–40 target brands** (NG DTC/e-commerce, fintech apps, skincare, food, SaaS) and reach out directly.
- **White-glove their first campaign:** you personally help write the brief and match creators. This guarantees a great first experience and surfaces product gaps.
- Lean on the **anti-AI / verified-human** angle and **escrow safety** as the pitch.

**Content/SEO engine starts:** publish niche/location landing pages + a few high-intent guides ("best UGC creators in Nigeria", "how UGC escrow works") for SEO/AEO/GEO compounding.

**Success check (end of W8):** 150+ creators; 10+ brands onboarded; 10–20 **real paid** gigs through escrow; time-to-first-hire measured; first reviews collected.

## Phase 3 — Weeks 9–12: Real revenue + repeat loop
**Demand scale:** open brand self-serve. Run paid acquisition tests (Meta/TikTok) now that Pixel + landing pages exist. Keep concierge for high-value brands.

**Repeat-business machine:**
- Prompt brands to **re-hire winners** and leave reviews; **syndicate reviews** (Trustpilot/Google) for trust + SEO.
- Turn on **featured placement/boosts** (bounded) and begin testing the first **"fuel money"** micro-fee (e.g., instant-payout) — introduced with clear notice.
- Start the **weekly tier ladder** (flagged on) once there's enough gig volume to make standings meaningful.

**Success check (end of W12):** 300+ creators; 30+ active brands; measurable **repeat purchase rate**; first platform revenue (12% brand fee) flowing; NPS collected both sides.

## North-star & weekly metrics to watch
- **GMV of approved gigs** (north star), # verified creators, niche coverage, active brands, **time-to-first-hire**, AOV, **repeat purchase rate**, delivery success rate, dispute rate, payout success, NPS, infra cost/active user.

## Founder tasks that can't be delegated
- Buy domain (Namecheap→Cloudflare); set up Paystack, Smile ID/Dojah, Supabase, Vercel, GA4/Clarity/Meta/PostHog accounts (I'll flag timing).
- Send the **escrow brief to your lawyer** (PRD §12.2) — gates real-money gigs.
- Personally recruit the first creator cohort and concierge the first brands (founder-led growth is the moat early).

## What we deliberately DON'T do
- No waitlist / "coming soon" page.
- No big paid ad spend before supply density + a proven loop.
- No celebrity/influencer chasing (we sell content assets, not reach).
- No turning on every gamification feature before there's volume to make it feel real.
