## 1. How "Escrow" works here
- When a Brand hires a Creator, the Brand pays the Creator's rate **plus the platform/protection Fee** ([FEE_PERCENT], min [FEE_MIN]) **upfront**.
- These funds are **held in a protected state via our licensed payment provider, [PAYMENT_PROVIDER]**, and are **not released to the Creator** until the release conditions in §3 are met. ⚖️ **[COMPANY] does not take custody of user funds as a principal; funds are handled through [PAYMENT_PROVIDER]'s regulated facilities / a licensed escrow arrangement. Counsel to confirm the exact mechanism and that no additional CBN/PSSP licence is required — or to specify the licensed structure.**
- "Escrow" is used descriptively for this hold-and-release flow; it does not imply a statutory escrow unless a licensed escrow provider is used.

## 2. No off-platform payment
Escrow protection applies **only** to payments made through the Platform. Paying or receiving outside the Platform voids this protection (Terms §6).

## 3. Release of funds
Funds (less Fees) are released to the Creator's verified bank account when:
(a) the **Brand approves** the delivery; or
(b) the **review window expires** (default **[7] days** after a conforming delivery is submitted) **without** the Brand approving or opening a dispute, and our review finds the delivery **materially conforms to the written Brief**; or
(c) a **dispute** is resolved in the Creator's favour (§5).
On release, final unwatermarked files unlock for the Brand. Target payout: **next business day** via [PAYMENT_PROVIDER]. Payout timing may vary with provider/bank processing.

## 4. Fees
The **[FEE_PERCENT]** platform/protection Fee is payable by the Brand and is **earned when the Gig is funded/facilitated**. It covers escrow protection, verified delivery, and dispute handling. The Fee is **non-refundable** except where a Gig is cancelled before work begins or as required by law. ⚖️ *Confirm Fee refundability scenarios and VAT.*

## 5. Disputes
- Either party may open a dispute within the review window via the Platform. Escrow remains **held** during a dispute.
- Disputes are decided **against the written Brief**, using the on-Platform record (Brief, drafts, time-stamped feedback, messages), not on who argues hardest.
- Possible outcomes: **full release** to the Creator, **partial release**, a **mandated revision**, or **refund** to the Brand (full or partial). The platform Fee may be retained, refunded, or pro-rated per the outcome. ⚖️ *Counsel to set Fee treatment per outcome.*
- [COMPANY]'s determination is **final for the release/refund of Escrow**, without prejudice to the parties' other legal rights. The full process is in the Dispute Resolution Policy.

## 6. Refunds & cancellations
- **Before work begins / before acceptance:** the Brand may cancel for a refund of the rate; Fee treatment per §4.
- **After delivery:** refunds only via approval or the dispute process.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible, subject to provider timelines. ⚖️ *Align with FCCPC consumer-protection requirements.*

## 7. Chargebacks & fraud
- Initiating a **chargeback** instead of using the dispute process is a breach; we may suspend the account, reverse releases where permitted, and recover amounts owed.
- We monitor for fraud (collusion, fake accounts, inflated view claims on per-view campaigns, chargeback abuse) and may hold, reverse, or forfeit funds and apply penalties, consistent with law and provider rules.

## 8. Wallets & credits
Where the Platform offers wallet balances or credits (e.g., unspent per-view campaign funds), these are **store-of-value credits usable on the Platform**, governed by separate wallet terms. ⚖️ *Counsel to confirm e-money/stored-value implications.*

## 9. Taxes
Amounts may be subject to **VAT/other taxes**; where required these are added and remitted per law. Creators are responsible for income tax on their earnings. ⚖️ *Tax adviser to confirm.*

## 10. Currency & provider
All amounts are in **Naira (₦)** unless stated. Payments and payouts are processed by **[PAYMENT_PROVIDER]**; your use is also subject to the provider's terms. We may add or change providers (e.g., Flutterwave, or a licensed escrow partner) and will keep the hold-and-release protections described here.

We may update these terms with notice; changes do not affect the release terms of Gigs already funded.
