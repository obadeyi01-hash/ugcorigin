## 1. Scope
This policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal data when you use UGCOrigin (the "Platform"). It applies to Creators, Brands, and visitors.

## 2. Data we collect
**You provide:**
- Account & profile: name, email, phone, password/social sign-in identifiers, avatar, bio, niches, location, links.
- Creator specifics: portfolio/samples, rates, social handles, **bank account details** (for payouts).
- Brand specifics: company name, website/store, business registration details.
- **Identity verification data (sensitive):** government ID, **BVN/NIN**, selfie/liveness data — processed via our verification provider(s) ([KYC_PROVIDER]).
- Content you upload, messages, briefs, applications, reviews, and support requests.

**Collected automatically:**
- Device, browser, IP, approximate location, pages/actions, and cookies/identifiers via analytics (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Meta Pixel) and our own logs. See §9.

**From third parties:**
- Sign-in data from Google/Meta/X (per your permission); verification results from [KYC_PROVIDER]; payment/payout status from [PAYMENT_PROVIDER]; limited public social profile data you link.

Subject to law, you may: access your data; correct it; request deletion; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent (e.g., marketing); request portability; and lodge a complaint with the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)**. ## 9. Cookies & tracking
We use necessary cookies (to run the Platform and keep you signed in) and, **with your consent**, analytics/marketing cookies (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Meta Pixel). You can manage preferences via our cookie banner and your browser. A separate **Cookie Policy** details each cookie. ⚖️

## 10. Security
We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and least privilege, Row-Level Security on our database, tokenization/minimization of sensitive fields, audit logging, rate limiting and bot protection, and staff MFA. No system is perfectly secure; we maintain a breach-response process and will notify affected users and the NDPC where required.

## 11. Children
The Platform is for users **18+**. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if we learn we have, we delete it.
